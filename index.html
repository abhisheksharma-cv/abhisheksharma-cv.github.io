I'm Abhishek. I build computer vision and multimodal AI systems, from research ideas to real-world deployment.

My interests center on vision-language models and multimodal LLMs: how they perceive, remember, and reason about a changing world. My work spans streaming video, medical imaging, 3D perception, and efficient edge inference. At Amazon, I develop personal and ambient understanding for Alexa devices. I’m also exploring vision-language-action models for robotics.
